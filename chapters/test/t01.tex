A \vocab{least-squares line} minimises squared residuals.
\begin{theorem}[Slope]
	$\hat b = \sum (x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)/\sum(x_i-\bar x)^2$.
\end{theorem}

	Slope $\gv{t01_demo/slope}$, intercept $\gv{t01_demo/intercept}$.
\end{example}
